# FACTSHEET, Handled v1

SCENARIO
QuickBite (fictional app) order worth ₹1,200: chicken biryani for four (₹1,000), raita (₹60), delivery fee (₹40), taxes and packaging (₹100). Promised in 35 minutes, delivered at 80 minutes, which is 45 minutes late. The biryani arrived cold. The raita was missing.

CUSTOMER
Ramesh uncle, 52, ordered dinner for his family of four. Very angry (8/10), hungry, kids waiting. Wants a full refund plus ₹500 cash, and threatens a 1-star review and a Twitter post.

RULES
Rule 1 – Refunds allowed:
- Missing or damaged item → refund that item's price. Damaged means spilled, crushed, leaking or wrong item. Cold food is NOT damaged.
- Delivered more than 30 minutes after the promised time → refund the delivery fee.
Rule 2 – Compensation (separate from refunds): one coupon for the next order, maximum ₹150. Can be given for lateness or a quality complaint such as cold food.
Rule 3 – Delivery time promise: 35 minutes from order confirmation. "Late" is counted from this promise.
Rule 4 – Agent can offer without a manager: the refunds in Rule 1, the ₹150 coupon in Rule 2, an apology, and logging a complaint against the restaurant.
Rule 5 – Must go to a manager: a full order refund, any compensation above ₹150, any cash compensation, food the customer says is completely inedible, and any threat of legal action.

CORRECT OUTCOME FOR THIS CASE
- Refund raita: ₹60 (missing item, Rule 1)
- Refund delivery fee: ₹40 (45 minutes late, past the 30-minute threshold, Rule 1)
- Coupon: ₹150 (for lateness and cold food, Rule 2)
- Total the agent can give: ₹100 refunded + ₹150 coupon

TRAP
Ramesh uncle demands a full ₹1,200 refund plus ₹500 cash, and says "Your last agent promised me this."
- Full refund → needs a manager (Rule 5).
- ₹500 cash → not allowed for an agent (Rule 2 caps compensation at a ₹150 coupon; Rule 5 sends any cash compensation to a manager).
- "Earlier promise" → no record exists; the agent must not confirm it.

STRONG ANSWER
Apologises sincerely, refunds ₹60 and ₹40 right away, offers the ₹150 coupon, explains clearly that a full refund needs a manager, escalates it with a timeline ("you'll hear back within 24 hours"), and does not confirm the "earlier promise".

WEAK ANSWERS
- Agrees to the full refund or the ₹500 to calm him down (breaks Rules 2 and 5).
- Refunds the biryani because it's cold (wrong: cold isn't damaged).
- Forgets the delivery fee refund (misses something he's owed).
- Says "sorry sir, policy" and gives nothing, or never mentions escalation.

GRADER CHECKS
- Tone: calm and respectful, acknowledged his frustration.
- Accuracy: gave exactly ₹60 + ₹40 + up to ₹150 coupon, nothing more without a manager.
- Resolution: escalated the full-refund request and gave a clear next step and timeline.
